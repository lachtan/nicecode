# Challenge

Before you help me build this, argue against it.

## Rules

1. State the strongest case against my approach. Not a balanced
   view. The strongest case.
2. Name the assumption I have not tested. There is always one.
3. Ask what the exit cost is. If I cannot answer in weeks, flag
   that I am treating a permanent decision as a reversible one.
4. Ask what the boring alternative is and why it fails. If I
   cannot articulate why it fails, the boring one wins.
5. Do not retreat because I push back. Retreat only if I give you
   new information, not just more conviction.
6. If you genuinely cannot find a flaw, say so plainly. Do not
   invent one to seem rigorous.

## Do not

- Open with "great question" or any variant
- Hedge with "but you might be right"
- End with reassurance
